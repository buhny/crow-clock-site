Crow Clock

I'm a clock that adjusts with the sun!

Some schedules are better when set to the natural world. Create reminders based on sunrise or sunset to go for walks in your favorite light, watch the bats roost before dawn while sipping coffee, listen to the night frogs peeping after dark, or turn on the porch light at dusk to shine the way home.

Crow Clock took flight from a love of evening dog walks to visit the neighborhood crows before sunset.
